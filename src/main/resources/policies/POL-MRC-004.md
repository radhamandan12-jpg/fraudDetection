## 2.1 Scope
This procedure applies when the customer recognises the merchant but disputes
fulfilment, cancellation of a recurring service, or the charged amount. Goods or
services not received, cancelled subscriptions still billing, and incorrect
amounts are merchant disputes, not unauthorised use.

## 2.2 Merchant dispute handling
Classify MERCHANT_DISPUTE and route to DISPUTE_OPERATIONS when the customer
recognises the merchant and the issue concerns fulfilment, cancellation or
amount. Device and location anomalies are not expected and their absence does
not weaken the classification.

## 2.3 Evidence requirements
The recommendation must cite the intake statement, the transaction record and
the merchant risk profile. A merchant on the internal blocklist raises risk and
may justify FRAUD_INVESTIGATION even when the merchant is recognised.
