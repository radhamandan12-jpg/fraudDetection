## 1.1 Scope
This legacy procedure required analysts to place an outbound call to the
customer before any classification. It was retired when the evidence-driven
triage model replaced callback verification.

## 1.2 Withdrawn procedure
Do not apply this document to live cases. It is retained only to prove that
retired policy content never reaches the retrieval corpus or a recommendation.
